## 💼 Resume & Portfolio
Leverage resources at your local library for free resume-writing and ATS optimization.  I saw great results from leveraging JobScan, specifically.  You can partner with them by contacting [Isaac Taylor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/isaactlr/) and telling him I sent you. 

* [Jobscan.co](https://app.jobscan.co/dashboard) - Compare and generate resumes custom to the Applicant Tracking System (ATS) used for the posted Job Description. 
* [ResumeAI](https://withresumeai.com/) - Free ATS checker and AI resume builder (3 ATS checks/day with no account). Also [State of ATS 2026](https://github.com/Kayvan-Zahiri/state-of-ats-2026): 738 large employers, 704 portal-verified, Workday 37.9%.
* [Resumake.io](https://resumake.io) - Simple LaTeX resume generator.

* **4 months** applying = 1st Interview

### Networking Approach
**Focus conversations around helping others**, rather than them helping you.  The conversation will eventually gravitate towards your job search if you ask about them and what they do.  Focus on how you can help them succeed with their goals, and trust that they will reciprocate if they can. 

## Job Boards

# 🎖 Military & Veteran Career Resources
Work with Hope for the Warriors and leverage their partnership with JobScan to obtain a free Premium license to use the tool. 

* [Hope for the Warriors](https://www.hopeforthewarriors.org/): They helped me the most with my transition in 2026. 
* [Recruit Military](https://recruitmilitary.com/) : A platform focused on connecting veterans with employers.
* [Hire Heroes USA Job Board](https://jobs.hireheroesusa.org/): Career coaching and job placement for transitioning service members and veterans.
* [VetJobs](https://vetjobs.org/): Veteran-specific job board and employment assistance.
* [Hire Veterans](https://hireveterans.com/): A job board specializing in the veteran talent pool.
* [G.I. Jobs](https://www.gijobs.com/): Resources and job listings tailored to the military community.
* [Clearance Jobs](https://clearancejobs.com): A career network specifically for professionals with federal security clearances.
